Terms of Use

Purpose and access

Note Bridge is an experimental personal-use plugin for synchronizing an Obsidian vault with Google Drive. The configured authorization service is restricted to allowed accounts; this website does not offer unrestricted public registration. Use only accounts and files you are authorized to access, and comply with Google, Cloudflare and Obsidian terms applicable to your use.

Your responsibilities

Keep independent backups. Start with a test vault, review conflicts and check deletions before resolving them. Do not run another synchronizer against the same local vault without understanding the risks. Do not copy device credentials or synchronization state between devices. Keep service secrets and pairing codes private.

Limitations

The plugin is provided as experimental software without a guarantee of uninterrupted operation, preservation of every edit or suitability for a particular purpose. Google authorization can expire or be revoked. Offline edits and simultaneous changes may cause conflicts. iOS may suspend background work. A history snapshot in the same Drive account is not an independent backup.

Leaving the service

You can disconnect the device and revoke Google access at any time. Disconnecting does not delete notes or history. See the Privacy Policy for data retention, deletion and failed server-revocation details.

Note Bridge is an experimental Obsidian plugin for two-way synchronization between a local vault and ordinary Markdown files and attachments in a Google Drive folder.

What it does

Edit notes locally on your phone, tablet or computer. While Obsidian is active, the plugin checks for changes, transfers files and helps you review conflicting edits. Files added to the selected Drive folder by other applications can also be synchronized.

Why Google access is requested

Google sign-in identifies an allowed account. Drive access lets the plugin list, read, create, update and trash files to synchronize notes, folders and revision snapshots.

The current permission is broad. The plugin requests the Google Drive drive scope. The folder setting limits the plugin's behavior, not the authority of the Google token. Only connect an account and an authorization service that you trust.

How your data travels

Note contents move directly between Obsidian and Google Drive. A Cloudflare Worker handles authorization and stores encrypted refresh tokens in Cloudflare D1. This public website only explains the project; it does not sign you in or upload notes.

Private use, not an open registration service

The authorization service admits only explicitly allowed, verified Google email addresses and requires the service owner's pairing code. Each device has its own connection key. Google Drive sharing permissions determine which other accounts can access a folder.

Before using it

Keep an independent backup and try a test vault first. Synchronization can propagate deletions; simultaneous edits may require review. iOS may suspend synchronization when Obsidian is backgrounded.
